Meet LOCOboard

The ultimate Arduino expansion

LOCOboard simplifies Arduino robotics while keeping all the flexibility and compatibility makers love. Plug it on, skip the messy wiring, and stay free to add any custom hardware.

  • ⚡
    Instant connections: Dedicated ports for servos, motors, and sensors
  • 🎨
    Built‑in creativity: 8x8 LED matrix and RGB LEDs for rich visual feedback
  • 🔊
    Interactive: Onboard buzzer and buttons for playful input and output
